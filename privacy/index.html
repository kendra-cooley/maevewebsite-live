Privacy

LAST UPDATED JULY 28, 2026

The only personal detail Maeve Security collects is the email address you choose to give us. We use it to tell you when we launch and to send occasional news about the project. We don't sell it, we don't rent it, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the service that delivers those emails. We do count visits to the site, with open-source analytics we host ourselves — no cookies, and nothing that points back to you.

What we collect

Your email address — and only if you type it into the sign-up form and submit it. We don't ask for your name, your employer, or anything else.

Anonymous, aggregate visit statistics, described under Analytics below.

That's the whole list. The site sets no cookies, runs no advertising, and loads no third-party trackers or social pixels.

Why we collect it

To let you know when Maeve Security goes live, and to send occasional news about the project — new stories, writing, and events.

That is the only use. Your address is never used for anything you didn't sign up for.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell, rent, or trade email addresses, and we don't hand them to advertisers, data brokers, or partners.

The only third parties that ever see your address are the services we use to collect the sign-up and send the emails — currently Google Forms for the sign-up itself, and our email provider for delivery. They process it on our behalf, for that purpose alone.

The one exception is the law: if a valid legal order compels disclosure, we have to comply.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until the list is retired. There is no archive kept on the side.

Your choices

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes you off the list.

You can also write to us at any time to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We act on deletion requests promptly and don't ask for a reason.

Analytics

We do want to know whether anyone is reading, so the site counts visits — with Umami, an open-source analytics tool we run on our own server. Not Google Analytics, and nothing from the big ad platforms. The numbers stay with us and go to no one else.

It is cookieless: nothing is stored on your device, and there is no identifier that follows you between visits or across other sites. What it records is the page you landed on, the site that referred you, and rough browser, device and country information. Your IP address is used only to work out that approximate country, and is not stored.

None of it can identify you, and none of it is or can be connected to an email address on the sign-up list.

Other requests the page makes

The typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so loading a page fetches them from Google's servers, which sees the request the way any web server does. It sets no cookie here and gets nothing about you beyond that request.

Everything else — the styles, the images, the small script behind the sign-up form — comes from this site.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and the sign-up list sits in access-controlled accounts. No system is perfect — but the less we collect, the less there is to lose.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Anything that materially changes how we use your email will go to the list before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about any of this, or want your address removed? Email us at privacy@maevesecurity.com.

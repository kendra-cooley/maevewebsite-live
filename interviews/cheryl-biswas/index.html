EPISODE 01

Cheryl Biswas

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

24 AUGUST 2026 · 48 MIN

In the inaugural episode of the Maeve Security Podcast, threat intelligence researcher Cheryl Biswas shares her unconventional path from political science into cybersecurity. She opens up about burnout, mentorship, and community, and dives into her passion projects on critical infrastructure and Arctic cybersecurity sovereignty for Canada.

“If you could solve a problem because you are willing to try something and learn it, it's gonna take you places. Don't shut that out — go for it. You have no idea what you could unearth with that.”

Show notes

In this episode

  • Cheryl’s unconventional path into cybersecurity — from a political science degree to teaching herself tech, starting at the help desk
  • Being told early on she “couldn’t” learn computers, and proving that wrong
  • Her work today in threat intelligence: tracking APTs, ransomware, botnets, and software supply chain risk
  • Navigating a layoff, and lessons on resilience, burnout, and believing in yourself
  • The importance of community, mentorship, and conference speaking in building a security career
  • Work-life balance, burnout recovery, and the danger of isolation in the industry
  • Her passion projects on critical infrastructure resilience and Arctic cybersecurity sovereignty for Canada
  • Her role with the Diana Initiative supporting women and underrepresented groups in cybersecurity

“If I could do this, you can do this.”

Cheryl Biswas, Deborah Turner-Chappell & Bee Mallory

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

8 NOVEMBER 2026 · 52 MIN

Recorded live at Hackfest in Quebec City, Kendra sits down with Cheryl Biswas, Deborah Turner-Chappell and Bee Mallory to talk about first conferences, finding your people, and what it's really like to be a woman at a security conference. Plus hot takes on phishing simulations, security awareness month and AI.

“A lot of the time we say, oh, you sound like an AI. No, the AI sounds like us. It was made off of us.”

Show notes

In this episode

  • First conferences: Circle City Con, BSides Ottawa, and a BSides Toronto keynote given in high school
  • Feeling like you belong, and the people who made the difference
  • Two Hackfest 2026 talks: Arctic sovereignty, and Data Bullshit and Post-Truth
  • How Maeve started on the Hackfest floor two years ago
  • Being mistaken for a plus one, asked “Why are you here?”, or overlooked as a mature woman
  • What conference organizers get wrong: manels, invisible policies and incident response
  • Hot takes on phishing simulations, Security Awareness Month and AI
  • Lightning round, plus where to find each guest

“It is the tool. Don’t be the tool.”

EPISODE 05

Michelle Croak

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

20 OCTOBER 2026 · 53 MIN

Independent GRC consultant Michelle Croak on why her work is really about people: stumbling into IT general controls, going independent, how reading fiction made her a better risk manager, being the only woman in the room, and why you should like who you become at work.

“I think it's not only if you like it, you should do it, but you should also like the version of you that comes out when you do it.”

Show notes

In this episode

  • Michelle’s path from a tiny college risk management major to realizing “this is just how my brain works”
  • Building an IT general controls program from scratch, starting with a quick Google
  • Why GRC is people management when zero of the people report to you
  • Going from in-house to independent consulting, and the art of the possible
  • How reading fiction made her more empathetic with difficult stakeholders
  • Her CRISC prep course on Cybrary, and why AI won’t replace the people who get eight stakeholders to agree
  • Being the only woman in the room, and staying yourself through it
  • Her advice for newcomers: like the version of you that comes out at work

“Being a woman in the field is not easy, but it’s necessary.”

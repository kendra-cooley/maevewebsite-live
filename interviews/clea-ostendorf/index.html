EPISODE 04

Clea Ostendorf

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

29 SEPTEMBER 2026 · 35 MIN

In this episode of the Maeve Security Podcast, Kendra sits down with Clea Ostendorf, co-founder and CEO of Wolfpack Security, to talk about her winding path from tech staffing into cybersecurity and the leap of faith it took to build her own company. Clea gets candid about balancing founder life with family and friendships, why "human in the loop" matters more than ever in the age of AI, and what it's really like navigating an industry that doesn't always take you seriously at first. It's a conversation about resilience, expertise, and leaning into whatever skills make you, you.

“I always thought you had to be incredibly technical to succeed in this space. The more I learn, the more I believe it's not the vertical you need to think about — it's the underlying skills.”

Show notes

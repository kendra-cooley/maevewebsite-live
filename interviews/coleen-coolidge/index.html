EPISODE 03

Coleen Coolidge

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

24 AUGUST 2026 · 42 MIN

Two-time CISO Coleen Coolidge joins Maeve to share how she stumbled into cybersecurity while analyzing mortgage data, and how a mentor changed her path. She talks candid CISO life, burnout, women in security, unexpected allies in sales teams, and why the best security programs are true team sports.

“If I cared the most, I would make sure that it got done correctly.”

Show notes

In this episode

  • Coleen’s accidental entry into security through a mortgage-data project and a mentor named Ian
  • Life as a two-time CISO: organizational awareness, advocacy, and “day-to-day politicking”
  • “Retiring” from security — and getting pulled right back in as a field CISO
  • Her people-first philosophy: why security is ultimately about relationships, not just technical skill
  • Unwinding through Security Padel, running, and pushing outside her comfort zone
  • The philosophy that great security programs are a team sport, with every discipline rowing together
  • What outsiders misunderstand about security’s real job as risk finder and risk mitigator
  • The unexpected, powerful partnership between security and sales teams
  • Reflections on being a woman in cybersecurity over the last 20+ years

“Security really is about people.”

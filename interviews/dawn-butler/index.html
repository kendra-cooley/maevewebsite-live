EPISODE 02

Dawn Butler

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → (opens in a new tab)

24 AUGUST 2026 · 52 MIN

Dawn Butler, longtime Citi cybersecurity leader and host of the podcast Bytes, Bits, and Brilliance, joins Maeve to share her path from soldering circuits to cybersecurity governance. She opens up about burnout, layoffs, therapy, and rediscovering herself beyond work, plus hard-won advice on visibility, leadership, and representation in tech.

“You are brilliant, but no one knows you.”

Show notes

In this episode

  • Dawn’s winding path from electrical engineering and software development into cybersecurity governance
  • Teaching herself new skills on the fly — including learning Oracle databases in a single intense weekend
  • Her first physical burnout, tendinitis, and the toll of years of overwork
  • Getting laid off in 2024 while on medical leave, and the emotional unraveling that followed
  • “From Pause to Power,” her healing circle for women navigating unemployment and career transitions
  • Rediscovering identity beyond “Work Dawn” — meditation, reading, prayer, and travel as coping tools
  • A proud leadership moment: building “the little team that could” and empowering the women on it
  • Her core career advice: the importance of visibility, even for introverts
  • The ongoing need for greater representation of women and people of color in cybersecurity

“Come on out, be visible.”

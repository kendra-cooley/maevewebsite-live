The mentors I never met

9 APRIL 2026 · BY NADIA COLE · 5 MIN

Building a career from talks, blogs, and borrowed courage.

Placeholder body. Replace with the real essay.

I did not have a mentor. I had a browser history. For about four years, every person who taught me anything about this field did so without knowing I existed — through a conference talk uploaded eighteen months earlier, a blog post with nine comments, a thread that answered exactly the question I had been too embarrassed to ask.

Borrowed courage

The thing I took from them was not really technical. It was the evidence that someone had stood up and said the thing anyway.

That is available to anyone with a search bar, which is both the good news and the reason I now answer my own inbox.
